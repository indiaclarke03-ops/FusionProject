Supplier Check: pick a company and see what sits upstream of it.

This working prototype runs on the evidence behind this site: the systems map, Sayari records and the harm ratings. It follows every recorded or reported flow back towards the mine, flags the risks it finds, and tells a buyer what to do about each one.

How it works

Seven parts, each covering a gap the others leave.

No single tool can follow a metal from mine to magnet. Company and customs data stops at the processing plant; paperwork can be faked; lab tests need samples; satellites see mines but not buyers. Combined, and made a condition of public money, they close most of the gap.

    Tested, not assumed

    We ran the screening layer on real tape and wire makers. It found the Moscow route and the sanctions links, and no mines.
