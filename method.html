08 · Method & limits

How this site knows what it claims, and where it doesn't

What this site does

The Magnet Problem traces the materials in five parts of a generic fusion reactor back through their supply chains to the mine, and shows the documented human rights findings at each stage next to the supply-security facts that usually frame the conversation. It argues that fusion's supply chain should be planned as a people problem first, and that the pre-commercial stage is the cheapest moment to build traceability in.

How the trace works

  • Stages. Each material is laid out from the reactor back to the mine: reactor or developer, component maker, metal, oxide or alloy, separation or refining, and mine or source region. Where a chain has more than one source, it splits into routes.
  • Lens toggle. The security lens shows concentration, control, export restrictions and single points of failure, coloured by supply risk. The people lens shows human rights findings on the same stages, coloured by harm severity. Supply-risk colours are the author's judgement from the cited evidence; harm colours follow the rule below.
  • Evidence levels. Every stage carries a badge, described below. A stage can carry two: one for harm at the source, one for the link into fusion's supply chain.
  • Harm rating (people lens colour). A stage is rated severe when three or more sources document harm there, or when the documented harm is extreme (forced labour, child labour, or any crime against people, such as killings, violence against civilians or armed-group abuses), even if only one source shows it. One or two sources means moderate. A stage with no documented harm is shown as such, not as safe. Sayari risk flags never count as documented harm: they are modelled indicators. Each rated stage says why it got its rating.
  • Blending points. Marked where ore from different sources is mixed (at separation plants, smelters or export hubs) and origin can no longer be traced.

Data sources

  • Sayari: corporate registries, ownership, sanctions and watchlists, and customs trade records. It is the main source for which companies sit at each stage and how goods moved between them. Every Sayari finding on the site names the entity, links to it in the Sayari graph and is marked Confirmed (backed by a record such as a shipment or registry filing) or Inferred (a modelled or possible link, including Sayari's "possible supply adjacency" and sub-tier risk indicators). Risk flags are quoted by their Sayari name and are modelled indicators, not findings about a company's conduct.
  • Tavily: web research on NGO, UN, government and news sources, company filings (stock-market prospectuses, SEC conflict-minerals reports, annual reports) and peer-reviewed papers. Every cited page was opened, not taken from search snippets.
  • Public reports: every document cited on the site, listed below.

    What the data cannot show

    • Sayari shows recorded relationships and trade flows, not the conditions inside a mine.
    • Trade data loses origin at blending points. After a separation plant or smelter, ore from a conflict area and ore from elsewhere look the same on paper.
    • The absence of a link is not proof that there is no link.
    • Every source has a date. Mines open, close and change hands; findings may have moved on since publication.
    • Sayari's trade coverage is uneven. It holds no Myanmar-to-China rare earth or tin ore records, so the Kachin and Wa State routes rest on NGO and press sources. Japan publishes no shipment-level import data, so what Japanese tape makers buy is invisible. Domestic sales (for example within the US) do not appear in customs data.
    • A "one-hop" link to a sanctioned party means Sayari records a direct relationship; where the goods behind it were not retrieved, the site says so.

    Evidence rules

    • No claim without a public source. Claims still waiting for one are shown with a visible Source needed flag rather than removed or hidden.
    • Harm at a source is kept separate from proof of a link to fusion. A mine can have documented abuses while the route from that mine into a fusion magnet remains a likely pathway or a hypothesis.
    • Stages that have not been researched are shown as greyed Not yet researched nodes, never filled in.

    How AI was used

    Research was assisted by Claude using Tavily and the Sayari MCP. The site was built with Claude Code in VS Code. The design brief was generated with the author's own tool, Dress Code. All claims were reviewed by the author.
