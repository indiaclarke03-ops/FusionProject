03 · Risk tracer

Follow the risk from a mine or smelter to fusion, and back.

Risk teams usually start from a smelter list or a mineral. Pick one and the tracer follows every recorded, reported or suspected flow downstream to the fusion developers it could reach. Or start from a developer and work upstream to the harm sites behind it.

How the tracer grades a route

A route is only as strong as its weakest link. If a mine-to-smelter flow is confirmed in customs records but the smelter-to-wire-maker flow is only a hypothesis, the whole route is a hypothesis. The tracer always shows the strongest route available, and names the weak link that holds it back.

  • Confirmed record: a shipment or registry record in Sayari.
  • Reported: stated by a credible source, such as a company filing, NGO investigation or government report.
  • Inferred: follows from market structure or a model, for example where one plant processes most of a country's ore.
  • Hypothesis: plausible but untested.

This works the same way as a supplier's smelter list (a conflict minerals or extended minerals reporting template): it starts at the processing plant, where origin is usually lost, and asks what sits on either side. A missing route is not proof that no route exists; it means no record was found.
